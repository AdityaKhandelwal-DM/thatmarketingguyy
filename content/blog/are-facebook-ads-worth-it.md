Are Facebook ads worth it? For many small businesses, yes. For some, no, and I'd rather tell you that before you spend a dollar. I'm Aditya, a solo Meta Ads and Google Ads consultant working remotely from India with owners overseas. I've managed 89 ad accounts, so this answer comes from accounts I've actually run, not from theory.

## Is it worth paying for ads on Facebook?

It is worth it when a customer is worth clearly more to you than the cost of finding one, and you can follow up on leads fast. If either of those is missing, paying for ads usually isn't.

Facebook and Instagram ads put your offer in front of people who weren't searching for you. That suits businesses like restaurants, venues, clinics, D2C brands and local services. It suits them less if your product is cheap, your margin is thin, or nobody answers the phone.

Do one sum before anything else. What is one new customer worth to you? If that is $400, paying $40 to get a lead who becomes a customer one time in five is fine. If it's $15, the maths is much harder.

## Are Facebook ads effective?

Yes, when the offer is good and the tracking is honest. Facebook ads are effective at one thing in particular: reaching a lot of the right local people cheaply and testing which message gets a response.

They're not effective at fixing a weak product, a slow website or a sales team that never calls back. I've seen a hair transplant clinic's conversation ads fall from ₹93 per chat (about $1.11) to ₹33 (about $0.39) by changing the creative and targeting. The ad didn't get "better" in a vague way. It stopped attracting people who were never going to book.

## When Facebook ads are NOT worth it

I'd tell you to wait or look elsewhere if:

- **Your budget is tiny.** If you can't afford enough spend to collect results and judge them, you're guessing.
- **People already search for you.** If buyers type exactly what you sell into Google, [Google Ads](/google-ads-expert) may be the better first move. I compare them in [Facebook Ads vs Google Ads for a local business](/blog/facebook-ads-vs-google-ads-for-local-business).
- **You can't follow up.** Leads that wait three days go cold.
- **You want a guarantee.** Nobody honest can promise one.

## How do I know if they are working for me?

Judge them on cost per lead or cost per sale, never on likes or reach. Ask for a written report on a fixed schedule that shows spend, leads or sales, and cost for each.

Give a campaign a fair run before deciding. Meta's system needs a steady flow of results to settle, so the first couple of weeks on a small budget are mostly learning.
