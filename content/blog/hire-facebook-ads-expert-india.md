If you're a business owner in the US, UK, UAE, Australia or Singapore and you're thinking of hiring a Facebook ads expert in India, you probably have four worries: will we be able to talk, will I see what's happening, will I keep my ad account, and what's the catch with the lower price? I'm Aditya, a solo Meta Ads and Google Ads consultant working remotely from Jaipur. Here are straight answers.

## How do time zones work with a remote ads manager?

Mostly they don't get in the way, because ad management is async work. I build, test and optimise campaigns during my working day, and you read the update when yours starts.

Calls still happen. India is ahead of the UAE by 1.5 hours, ahead of Singapore by 2.5 and ahead of the UK by 4.5 to 5.5, depending on daylight saving. For the US and Australia the overlap is narrower, so we agree one or two call windows a week that suit you, early morning or evening on my side.

Day-to-day questions go over WhatsApp or email. I already work with clients across the US, UK, UAE, Australia and Singapore, and reporting that is clear enough means you never have to wait for a call to know what's happening.

## How will I get reports?

On a fixed schedule, in writing, and led by the numbers that connect spend to money: cost per lead, cost per chat or ROAS. Not reach and impressions on their own.

A good report answers three things: what you spent, what you got for it, and what changes next. I'd also give you read access to the same dashboard I'm looking at, so you can check any number yourself.

## Do I keep full access to my ad account?

Yes, and this is the one I'd never compromise on. I work through partner access on your Meta Business Manager. Your ads, audiences, pixel data and history stay in your account, so if we stop working together there's nothing to hand back.

Be wary of anyone who wants to run your ads from their own account. If they leave, or you fall out, your history leaves with them.

## Is it cheaper, and what's the catch?

Usually yes. An India-based freelancer typically costs a fraction of a US or UK one for similar work, because living costs here are lower. I'm not going to quote a price list in a blog post, because the right fee depends on your market and spend. Ask for a written quote and compare what's included.

The catch is real, and there are three parts:

1. **The time-zone gap.** Fast replies are possible, but a live chat at 2pm your time may not happen.
2. **One person.** I'm solo, so you get me directly, but there's no team behind me if I'm ill. Ask any freelancer how they cover that.
3. **Your market is different from mine.** Most of my numbers come from India, where clicks and leads cost far less than in the US or UK. Don't expect the same prices in your country.

That last point is worth saying plainly, because it's where overseas buyers get misled. My case studies show what I do with a budget. They don't promise your cost per lead. As a hypothetical example, a lead that costs a few cents in one market could cost many dollars in another.

It makes sense if you want direct access to the person building your campaigns, you're comfortable with async updates, and you like the idea of paying less for the same hands-on work. It makes less sense if you need an in-person team or a call every morning.
