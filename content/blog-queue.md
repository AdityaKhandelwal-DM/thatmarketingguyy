**Read `content/blog-briefs/RULES.md` first.** A row may only be written if it
passes all the topic gates there at the moment of writing (rows marked `hold`
or `research` are never written). Take the first row whose Status is `todo`,
that passes the gates, **and that has no open pull request yet** (check `gh pr list --state open`; skip any row whose slug already has an
open `blog/<slug>` PR), write it (rules in content/blog/README.md), then change
its Status to `done YYYY-MM-DD` in the same commit.

From row 16 on, every row has a **brief** in `content/blog-briefs/<slug>.md`:
meta title, meta description, H1, keywords, outline, FAQ, proof points, links
and images are decided there. Build the post from the brief; the shared rules
are in `content/blog-briefs/README.md`.

**Goal (Oct 2026 – Mar 2027): win international clients.** The reader is a
small-business owner in the US, UK, UAE, Australia or Singapore who is
deciding whether to hire someone to run their Facebook or Google Ads, and who
that should be. Every post should leave them trusting Aditya enough to open
/results and then /contact. Learning / DIY content is parked until ~Apr 2027
(see "Later" at the bottom).

Google's top results for bare "facebook ads expert / google ads expert for
hire" are marketplaces (Upwork, Fiverr, Toptal, Arc). These posts go after the
questions buyers ask *before* hiring — cost, agency vs freelancer, red flags,
hiring offshore, niche specialists — where a real practitioner can outrank a
marketplace.
