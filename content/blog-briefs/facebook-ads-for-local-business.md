## FAQ

- ### Are lead forms or chat ads better for a service business? — Chat when the job needs a quick quote; forms when you need details first.
- ### How big should my target radius be? — The area you'd actually drive to, plus a little; don't go state-wide.
- ### How fast should I follow up on Facebook leads? — Same hour; leads cool fast.
