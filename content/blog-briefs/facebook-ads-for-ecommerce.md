## FAQ

- ### Are Facebook ads good for ecommerce? — Yes when margin and creative support it; best channel for products people don't search for yet.
- ### What is a good ROAS for Facebook ads? — Above your break-even ROAS (1 ÷ margin); explain in one line.
- ### How much should a small ecommerce store spend on Facebook ads? — Enough to get ~50 purchases a month per ad set to learn; start with a test.
