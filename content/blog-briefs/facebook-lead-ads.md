## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- Lead ads keep people inside Facebook/Instagram (instant forms), so volume is easy and quality is the problem: form type, questions and follow-up decide results.
- Different from the 'not working' and 'cost' posts: this is a step-by-step setup guide for the lead-ad format itself, for service businesses.
- Tying leads to WhatsApp/CRM and calling back fast matter more than in any other format.

## Proof and sources (only these figures)

- Career institute: video-first lead ads with creative per course: 2,766 leads at ₹34 (~$0.40).
- Boutique retreat: instant-form booking ads: 142 leads at ₹25 (~$0.30), CTR 3.61%.
- Rooftop lounge: reservation instant forms: 382 leads at ₹38; restaurant party bookings: 301 leads at ₹77; wedding resort: 562 leads at ₹138.
- Failures (/results cuts): venue on a Sales objective at ₹21,691 per lead → rebuilt as instant form at ₹45; nightlife venue at ₹2,264 per lead because form leads were never called back.
