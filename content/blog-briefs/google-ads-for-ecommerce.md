## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- The product feed (Merchant Center) is the campaign: titles, images, prices and stock decide where you show.
- Brand vs non-brand search must be split, or brand sales make the account look better than it is.
- Value is measured in ROAS against margin, not cost per lead; Performance Max needs clean conversion values.
