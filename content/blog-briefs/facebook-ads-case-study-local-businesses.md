## Proof and sources (only these figures)

- SOURCE 1 — Warsztat Fryzur Męskich (men's barbershop chain), Wrocław, Poland. https://www.facebook.com/business/small-business/success-stories/warsztat — Facebook and Instagram ads, Messenger, Facebook Jobs tool, pixel for online product sales; during closures shared 'how to trim your own hair' content. Results on page: 80% of new customers come from Facebook; 3,000 customers served per month; 28 employees across 3 salons; 140 applications and 2 hires via the Jobs tool; grew 'from a single barber's chair to 14 in three years'. Meta notes results are self-reported.
- SOURCE 2 — The Irish Gardener (garden design), Cork, Ireland. https://www.facebook.com/business/small-business/success-stories/the-irish-gardener — Facebook ads and boosted posts, Ads Manager, Meta Pixel, custom audiences, lookalike audiences, Messenger. Results on page: 86% of online-store traffic via Facebook and Instagram; 80% of customer referrals attributed to Facebook; 4x revenue in the first 2 weeks of lockdown vs Sep–Dec 2019 with Facebook ads; 27x return on ad spend (a specific campaign) and 2.5–14x on average; 1.9 million people reached in 2021; 37.5% of online-store sales via Facebook and Instagram.
- SOURCE 3 — ORPI Douai (real-estate agency), Douai, France. https://www.facebook.com/business/small-business/success-stories/orpi — kept running Facebook and Instagram ads through a two-month closure and shared moving/staging advice on its Page. Results on page: 35% return on marketing investment year-on-year; 16 employees kept their jobs; 20 new sellers attributed to Facebook, described as 'a potential €200,000 gross income'.
- ADITYA'S OWN (from /results): Hair transplant clinic: conversation ads retuned for intent, cost per chat ₹93 → ₹33 (~$1.11 → ~$0.39), 12.5L reach, ₹92K spend.
