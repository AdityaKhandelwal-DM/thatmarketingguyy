## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- Guests come from somewhere else: target the cities and countries your guests travel from, not the hotel's own town.
- You sell packages and dates, not rooms: weekend breaks, weddings, daycations and off-season offers each need their own creative and timing.
- Retargeting is where bookings happen: people who viewed your site or videos but didn't book are the cheapest guests to win back from OTAs.

## Proof and sources (only these figures)

- Boutique retreat (new property, zero pipeline): instant-form booking ads with tight geo and interest layering: 142 leads at ₹25 (~$0.30), CTR 3.61%, ₹3.5K spend.
- Farm stay: creator-led video lead ads plus a broad audience build: 1,442 leads at ₹45 (~$0.54), CTR 1.79%, 3.4L reach.
- Wedding resort: always-on wedding lead ads plus a daycation package for the off-season: 562 leads at ₹138 (~$1.65).
