## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- Demand is tied to the clock and the calendar: lunch, dinner, weekends, festivals. Ads need dayparting and a reason to come *this* week.
- Hyper-local: most diners travel a few kilometres, so the radius and the creative ('2 minutes from X') matter more than interests.
- Different goals need different campaigns: walk-ins and awareness (reach), table reservations (instant forms / messages), party and group bookings (lead forms per outlet), delivery orders (traffic to the ordering app).

## Proof and sources (only these figures)

- Rooftop lounge: instant-form reservation ads replacing generic traffic pushes: 382 leads at ₹38 (~$0.45), CTR 1.82%, ₹15K spend.
- Restaurant party bookings: instant-form party enquiry ads built per outlet: 301 leads at ₹77 (~$0.92), CTR 2.03%, ₹23K spend.
- Banquet & restaurant: creator content routed into conversation ads: ₹9.92 (~$0.12) per chat, 4.5L reach, ₹14K spend.
- New café: local reach ads held at frequency 1.0: CPM ₹1.61, 2.1L reach on ₹336 (~$4) spend.
