## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- Inventory changes daily: a vehicle catalog feed powers dynamic ads that show the cars someone viewed (Meta offers automotive inventory ads in some markets; keep it general).
- Finance and price claims are regulated in many countries; ads must show required terms; keep claims accurate.
- The goal is a showroom visit or test drive; leads need same-day calls and a booking, and stock-specific ads beat generic brand ads.
