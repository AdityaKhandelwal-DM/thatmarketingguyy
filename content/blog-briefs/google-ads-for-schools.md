## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- Admissions run on a calendar: most enquiries arrive in a few weeks before deadlines, so budgets must follow the intake cycle.
- The searcher is often a parent, and searches are programme- or grade-specific plus location ("IB school near…", "MBA evening classes…").
- Broad course keywords attract job seekers, students looking for free notes and competitors' brand searches; negatives matter more than in most industries.
- Nonprofit schools may qualify for Google Ad Grants; most private schools won't (say: check eligibility, no promises).
