## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- Fitness ads fall under Meta's health and personal-attributes rules: no 'Are you overweight?' copy, no implied body judgements; weight-loss claims face extra restrictions.
- The product is a trial/intro offer and a booked session; video of real sessions and the trainer sells better than transformations.
- Very local (a few kilometres); retention, not the first session, decides whether ads pay off.
