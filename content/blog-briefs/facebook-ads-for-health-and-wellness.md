## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- Meta's rules bite here: ads can't imply they know a person's health condition or body ("Struggling with your weight?"), and weight-loss ads face extra restrictions. Keep it general and link Meta's Advertising Standards.
- The product is an appointment slot: intro offers and first-visit packages, plus a booking or chat flow, convert better than 'learn more'.
- Trust is visual: real therapists, rooms and members (with consent) beat stock images; reviews and credentials in the creative help.
