## Outline

- Intro (first 100 words): name the keyword, say who I am (solo consultant, 89 accounts, works remotely with overseas owners), promise: by the end you'll know what a useful consultation looks like and how to spot a sales call.
- **## What happens in a Google Ads consultation?** Snippet answer as numbered list: goals and margins → account access (read-only) → search terms check → conversion tracking check → plan with budget and first 30 days.
- **## What should I bring to a Google Ads consultation?** Bullets: what a customer is worth, current monthly spend, read-only access, which leads turned into sales, your service area.
- **## What a good consultant checks first** with H3s:
  - ### Is conversion tracking counting real enquiries?
  - ### Which search terms are you paying for?
  - ### Is the account in your name?
- **## How do I find a reliable Google Ads consultant for a small business?** 4 signals: shows real numbers including failures, asks about margins before budgets, gives you the account, explains in plain words.

## FAQ (becomes FAQPage schema)

- ### Is a Google Ads consultation free? — Many are; say mine is a free first look at the account, and what you get from it.
- ### How long does a Google Ads consultation take? — 30–45 minutes is typical for a first call; an audit takes longer.
- ### Do I need to give the consultant access to my account? — Read-only access is enough for a first look; never hand over ownership.
- ### What is a free Google Ads audit? — A review of tracking, search terms, structure and spend, written up with the 3–5 fixes that matter most.

## Proof points (only these)

- 89 ad accounts, ₹93.4L (~$112K) spend managed, ₹21.1L (~$25K) on Google, 79,212 leads and conversions.
