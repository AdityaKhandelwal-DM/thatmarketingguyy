## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- The goal is a visit, not a click: directions, calls and store visits are the conversions that matter.
- Multiple stores need separate campaigns or asset groups per location, otherwise you can't tell which store the ads helped.
- Product and stock matter: ads should feature what's actually on the shelf in that store, and seasonal peaks (festivals, holidays) drive most of the year's sales.
