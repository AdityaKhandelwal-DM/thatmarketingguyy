## FAQ

- ### Why are my Meta ads approved but not delivering? — Usually audience size, caps or budget versus goal.
- ### How long should I wait before changing a Facebook ad that isn't spending? — Give a fixed check after 24–48 hours; don't edit hourly.
- ### Can a low budget stop Meta ads from spending? — Yes when the goal needs more data than the budget can buy (purchases vs leads).
- ### Why can't I see my own Facebook ad? — You may not be in the audience; use Ads Manager preview instead.
