## FAQ

- ### When should a school start admission ads? — 8–12 weeks before the intake deadline, with a smaller always-on campaign the rest of the year.
- ### What is a good cost per admission enquiry? — Depends on fee and conversion to enrolment; work back from lifetime fee value (hypothetical labelled).
- ### Are Facebook lead forms or website forms better for schools? — Lead forms for volume; add qualifying questions so admissions isn't swamped.
