## FAQ

- ### What is a good monthly budget for Google Ads for a small business? — Work backwards from cost per enquiry; no universal number.
- ### How long does Google Ads take to work? — Clicks in days; judge after a few weeks of data.
- ### What are the downsides of Google Ads? — You pay per click whether they buy or not; stops when you stop paying; competitive niches cost more.
