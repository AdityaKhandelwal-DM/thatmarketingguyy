## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- Legal keywords are among the most expensive clicks there are, so one wrong match type or missing negative wastes real money fast.
- Each practice area is its own market (family, immigration, injury, criminal): separate campaigns, ads and pages, and different hours (criminal and injury enquiries come at night).
- Intake decides the result: calls must be answered and logged; Local Services Ads (Google Screened, where available) charge per lead and need verification; advertising rules depend on your bar or regulator.
