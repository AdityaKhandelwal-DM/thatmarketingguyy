## Outline

- Opening (do not start with "I'm Aditya"): start from the owner's problem, paying OTAs commission on guests who would have booked direct. Keyword in the first sentence.
- **## How should a hotel run Google Ads?** numbered: brand-name search campaign (protect your own name from OTAs bidding on it), local intent search ("hotel near [landmark]"), Business Profile + calls/directions, track bookings/calls/directions as conversions, ad schedule and budgets by season.
- **## Why hotels should bid on their own name** (OTAs and metasearch show up for your brand; a cheap brand campaign keeps the direct booking).
- **## Google Hotel Ads and free booking links** short, neutral: these run through Google's hotel products and usually need your booking engine connected; check with your booking engine provider. No fee claims.
- **## What I count as a result for a hotel** bullets: direct bookings, calls, direction requests, booking-engine starts; cost per each.
- **## A real hotel campaign** proof section.

## FAQ (FAQPage schema)

- ### How much should a hotel spend on Google Ads? — Work back from average booking value and the OTA commission you'd save; start with brand + one local campaign.
- ### Do Google Ads work for small hotels? — Yes for brand protection and nearby-intent searches; less for broad "hotels in [city]" against OTAs.
- ### Should a hotel use Performance Max? — It can help with calls and directions when conversion tracking is right; the hotel on my results page ran Performance Max with Search.

## Proof points (only these)
