## Proof and sources (only these figures)

- SOURCE 1 — Lucy & Yak (clothing; first store in Brighton, 2019), UK. https://business.google.com/uk/resources/success-stories/lucy-and-yak/ (June 2023) — Shopping and Search from 2018, Performance Max by summer 2022, broad match expanded, separate paid and organic search objectives. Results on page: 233% increase in revenue YoY; 209% increase in traffic YoY; 54% of orders through paid Search from new customers; moving to Performance Max drove a 32% uplift in revenue.
- SOURCE 2 — Specscart (online eyewear, Manchester-born), UK. https://business.google.com/uk/resources/success-stories/specscart/ (November 2026) — moved to high-intent Search in 2021, split budget into brand and sales campaigns, then Performance Max once demand was captured; daily spend rhythm adjustments. Results on page: 10X brand growth; 3.8x ROAS; 122,000 orders; 76% of total sales by 2024; £4 million revenue through Google Ads; brand campaigns 8.5x ROAS, sales campaigns 3.8x ROAS; 22% efficiency gain from rhythm adjustments.
- SOURCE 3 — Bird & Blend Tea Co. (began at local food markets in 2012), Brighton, UK. https://business.google.com/uk/resources/success-stories/bird-and-blend/ (July 2024; data provided by the business) — Performance Max plus increased Search investment. Results on page: ROAS of 439% in 2023; 320% growth in new-customer sales since scaling Google Ads in 2020; 200% brand growth in two and a half years; 20–25% of online revenue from Google Ads.
- AADITYA'S OWN (from /results): Dry fruit retail, three stores: per-store Search + Performance Max with store-visit tracking: 4,050 store actions at ₹25.62 (~$0.31), ₹1.04L spend.
