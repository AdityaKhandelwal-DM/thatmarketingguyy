## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- Only some practice areas suit Meta (family, immigration, estate planning, injury awareness); people rarely buy legal help from a feed impulsively, so content and lead forms do the work.
- Lawyer advertising is regulated by the bar or regulator in each jurisdiction (claims, testimonials, 'specialist' wording); keep it general and tell readers to check their own rules.
- Intake screening is essential: forms must qualify case type and location, and leads must reach intake within the hour.
