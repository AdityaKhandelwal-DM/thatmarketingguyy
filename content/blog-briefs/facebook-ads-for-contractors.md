## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- High-ticket, slow decisions: people take weeks to choose, so retargeting and follow-up matter more than the first click.
- Lead quality beats lead volume: forms need qualifying questions (project type, budget range, timeline, postcode) or the crew wastes days on quotes.
- Proof sells: before-and-after photos and short job-site videos from your own work outperform stock creative; seasonality (roofing after storms, painting in dry months) shapes budgets.
