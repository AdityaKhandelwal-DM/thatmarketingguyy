## FAQ

- ### Why are my Google Ads getting clicks but no calls? — Search terms, landing page, or tracking; check in that order.
- ### Why are my Google Ads not showing? — Approval status, budget, bids, targeting; use the Ad Preview tool.
- ### How long before I judge a Google Ads campaign? — A few weeks and enough conversions to compare.
