## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- Every event has a deadline: budgets ramp up as the date nears and the ads stop the day it's over. Evergreen campaigns don't work.
- Two different products: one-off ticketed events (sell before a date) and venues that sell dates (enquiries for weddings, parties, corporate). They need different objectives.
- Past attendees and enquirers are the best audience: custom audiences and lookalikes from them beat interests.
