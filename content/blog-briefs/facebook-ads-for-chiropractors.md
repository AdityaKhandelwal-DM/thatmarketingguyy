## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- New-patient offers drive it (exam + adjustment specials), but some regions regulate how health offers are advertised; keep claims modest.
- Meta's personal-attributes rule: no 'Is your back pain ruining your life?' style copy that implies you know someone's condition.
- Value is in the care plan, not the first visit: follow-up speed and rebooking decide whether ads pay off; tight radius around the clinic.
