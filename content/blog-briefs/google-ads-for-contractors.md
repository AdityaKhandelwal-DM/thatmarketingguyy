## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- Planned, high-ticket projects (roof, remodel, landscaping), not emergencies: longer decisions, quote requests, and a few jobs a month can pay for everything.
- Seasonality and weather drive demand (storm damage, spring landscaping), so budgets and ads change by season.
- Lead quality matters more than volume: forms need project type, postcode and timeline; separate campaigns per service; Local Services Ads where available.
