## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- Two audiences with opposite searches: sellers ("what's my home worth", "sell house [area]") are usually the more valuable lead; buyers search by area and property type.
- Housing ads have extra rules: in some countries (e.g. the US and Canada) Google limits personalised targeting for housing ads, so location and keywords do the work. Keep it general and link Google's policy.
- Long, slow pipelines: a lead may transact months later, so CRM follow-up and tracking qualified appointments matter more than form counts.
