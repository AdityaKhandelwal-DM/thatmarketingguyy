## What's different about this industry (gate 7 — use as the 'What's different' section)

- Sensitive health category: Google limits personalised advertising for health topics, and some services (e.g. addiction treatment in some countries) need certification. Keep it general and link Google's policy.
- Privacy: tracking enquiries must avoid sending health information to ad platforms; use conversion events that don't reveal what someone is seeking help for.
- Searches are specific ("anxiety therapist near me", "couples counselling [city]", "online therapy"), and insurance/cash-pay wording changes who clicks.
