# Drop your photos here

Put raw photos in **this folder** — straight off the phone or camera, full
resolution, any orientation. Don't crop, resize, or compress them. Tell me when
they're in and I'll crop, colour-match, convert to WebP, and wire them into the
right slots.

Name each file with the slot name from the table below. Extension doesn't
matter (`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.heic` all fine).

## How to shoot them (this matters more than the camera)

- **Light**: stand facing a window, mid-morning or late afternoon. Never
  overhead tube light, never backlit against a bright window.
- **Phone is fine.** Portrait mode off — it mangles hair and glasses edges.
- **Background**: plain wall, your office, a café. Clear the clutter behind you.
- **Wear** what you'd wear to meet a client. Solid colours photograph better
  than busy patterns; avoid pure white and tight stripes.
- **Take 20+ of each.** Most will be unusable. That's normal.
- **Horizontal for scenes, vertical for you.**

## What I'll do with them

Crop to the exact ratios the layouts need, level the exposure so all photos on
a page look like one shoot, convert to WebP at quality 80, generate the
responsive sizes, write the alt text, and swap out the stand-in stock images.
The current Unsplash photos stay only until yours arrive.

## What I won't do

Put a stock photo of a stranger where your face should be. That's why the hero
is currently a desk shot and not someone else's portrait — until your photo is
here, the site says nothing about what you look like rather than something
false.
